Pulse Horizon

Lightweight Android Browser

  • Auto-clean: History, cookies, and cache are erased when you close the browser.
  • Small: About 12–15 MB of storage.
  • Smart cache: Cache stays around 20 MB and cleans itself while you browse.
  • Smart downloads: Files are saved with their correct names — the browser checks the real file type (PDF, ZIP, image, video, audio, documents). Saving happens only when you tap.
  • Desktop mode: The DESK button shows full desktop sites.
  • Full screen: The URL bar hides when a page opens. Double-tap the top of the screen to show it, double-tap again to hide it.
  • URL tools: Long-press the URL bar to Copy URL, Share URL, Paste and go, or open Help & Feedback.
  • Ad block: Known ad servers are blocked — faster pages, less data.
  • Zero background: No services, no notifications, no battery use after you exit.

How to use this browser

  • Searching the web. Type into the box on the home page and press go. Type an address instead of words and the site opens. You can also type straight into the address bar at the top at any time.
    Where: the search box on the home page, or the address bar.
  • Choosing your search engine. The browser searches with whichever engine you pick, and remembers it.
    Where: the Engine row just under the home page's search box.
    How: tap Engine, then tap Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Wikipedia. A tick marks the one in use.
  • Shortcuts. Up to ten tiles for the sites you use most. Each shows the website's own icon.
    Where: the grid under the search box on the home page.
    How: tap a tile to open it. Tap More to see all of them. Tap Edit to reveal a remove button on each tile and a + Add tile - tap Add, give it a name and an address, and it appears. Tap Done when finished.
  • Tabs. Keep several pages open at once and move between them.
    Where: the number button in the toolbar, beside the three-dot menu. The number is how many tabs are open.
    How: tap it to see your tabs, tap one to switch to it, and use New tab for a fresh one. Opening a link in a new tab: three-dot menu → Open in new tab.
  • Bookmarks. Save a page so you can find it again.
    Where: the three-dot menu.
    How: Bookmark this page saves the one you are on. Bookmarks lists everything you have saved - tap one to open it. Settings also has Clear all bookmarks.
  • Downloads. Save files, pictures and videos to your phone.
    Where: the three-dot menu → Downloads.
    How: when a page offers a file, the browser saves it and tells you when it is done. The Downloads list shows what you have saved and opens it when you tap it.
  • Blocking ads and trackers. Known ad servers are blocked so pages load cleaner and faster.
    Where: three-dot menu → Settings → Block ads and trackers.
    How: switch it on or off. Right below it, the browser shows how many have been blocked this session.
  • Desktop mode. Ask one site for its full desktop version instead of the mobile one.
    Where: three-dot menu → Desktop site.
    How: tap to turn it on for the tab you are on. It is remembered for that tab, so other tabs stay on the mobile version.
  • Dark or light look. Choose the theme you prefer.
    Where: three-dot menu → Theme, or Settings → Theme (tap to change).
    How: tap to cycle through the options. The whole browser changes at once.
  • Music visualiser (edge glow). A glow around the screen edges that moves with whatever is playing.
    Where: three-dot menu → Settings → Edge glow while media plays.
    How: switch it on, then play music or a video in the browser. It is off until you switch it on, and the browser warns you about the battery cost first.
  • Background music. Keep audio playing after you leave the browser.
    Where: three-dot menu → Settings → Background music.
    How: switch it on and a media notification appears with play, pause and skip controls. It stops the moment the music ends or you close the browser. The browser warns you about the battery cost before it starts.
  • Adding a song from your phone. Play a track you already have, with the visualiser following it.
    Where: three-dot menu → Music, or Settings → Add a song from this phone.
    How: tap it and choose the track from your phone.
  • Auto-hiding the address bar. The bar slides away while you read and comes back when you want it.
    Where: three-dot menu → Settings → Auto-hide the address bar.
    How: switch it on. The setting applies to the tab you are on.
  • Copying and sharing a link.
    Where: the three-dot menu.
    How: Copy link puts the address on your clipboard; Share sends it to another app.
  • Uploading a photo or a file. Some websites ask for a picture or a document.
    How: tap the site's own upload button and choose your camera or your files. The browser only asks for that permission at that moment, and only that one thing is shared.
  • When there is no internet. If a page cannot load, the browser says so plainly instead of leaving you on a blank screen.
    How: check your connection and tap Try again on the page.
  • Getting back to the home screen.
    Where: the house button at the left of the address bar.
    How: tap it from anywhere and you are back on the home screen.
  • Clearing space, and privacy on exit. The browser clears its cache and browsing data when you leave it, and Settings has Clear cache now for doing it by hand.
    Where: three-dot menu → Settings → Clear cache now.
  • Help & Feedback. Tell the developer what is broken or what you would like.
    Where: three-dot menu, or press and hold the address bar and choose Help & Feedback.
    How: write your message and send it.
